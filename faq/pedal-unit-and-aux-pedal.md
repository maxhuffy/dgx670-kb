# What can the [PEDAL UNIT] and [AUX PEDAL] pedals do (4 independent pedals? continuous/expression control?)
question: With a 3-pedal unit on [PEDAL UNIT] plus a pedal on [AUX PEDAL], do I get 4 independent pedals, which ones give continuous control, and can AUX take an expression pedal?
citations: [OM p.14], [OM p.15], [OM p.99], [RM p.14], [RM p.75–78]
verified: 2026-09-29

## From the manuals
- **Four independent assignments: yes.** The Pedal settings have slots 1 AUX ([AUX PEDAL]) and 2 Right, 3 Center,
  4 Left ([PEDAL UNIT]). Each is assigned separately [RM p.75].
  Path: [MENU] → Cursor buttons [▲][▼][◀][▶] Controller, [ENTER] → TAB [◀] Pedal [RM p.75].
- **Supported pedals:**
  - [AUX PEDAL]: the included footswitch, FC3A, FC4A or FC5 [OM p.14, p.99].
  - [PEDAL UNIT]: LP-1B/LP-1WH, which must be mounted on the L-300B/L-300WH stand [OM p.15, p.99].
- **Continuous control:**
  - Functions marked * (Pitch Bend Up/Down, Modulation) need the FC3A or the LP-1B/LP-1WH, and "proper operation
    cannot be done with a footswitch" [RM p.76–77].
  - Half Pedal (a Half Pedal Point for Sustain/Soft) works with the FC3A and **the right pedal of the LP-1B/LP-1WH**
    [OM p.15, RM p.76].
- **Up/down on one pedal: no.** Each pedal gets one function. Up and down are separate functions (Pitch Bend Up vs
  Down, Glide Up vs Down) [RM p.77]. Expression pedals (e.g. FC7) are not mentioned anywhere.
- **Logic types:**
  - Held: Sustain, Sostenuto, Glide.
  - Toggle/trigger: Style Start/Stop, Song Play/Pause, Tap Tempo, Modulation Alt.
  - Continuous: the * functions.
  [RM p.76–78]
- **Defaults:** AUX = sustain [OM p.14]. Unit: Right = Damper, Center = Sostenuto, Left = Soft [OM p.15].
- **Limitations:**
  - Center/Left functions are stored per Voice [RM p.14]. Set "Switch with Main Voice" to Off to keep your
    assignments when the Main Voice changes [RM p.76].
  - Priority when a pedal has several uses: Punch in/out → Registration Sequence → assigned function [RM p.75].
  - Don't press pedals at power-on (reverses polarity) [OM p.14–15]. AUX Pedal Polarity can reverse the AUX pedal
    [RM p.76].

## External information (NOT from the manuals, from the user's online research, unverified here)
- The FC35 3-pedal unit connects to [PEDAL UNIT]. The manuals name only the LP-1B/LP-1WH.
- On that unit, only the right (damper) pedal sends a continuous value; Center and Left are on/off only. The manuals
  limit Half Pedal to the right pedal [OM p.15] but do not say whether the * functions are limited to it [RM p.76].

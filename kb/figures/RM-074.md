# Figures on RM p.74 — Block Diagram
source: DGX-670_reference_manual_En_B0.pdf p.74 | described: 2026-09-29 | by: claude-opus-5-5
summary: f1 is the Mixer > Compressor "User Master Compressor" Save display (Type list User1–User8, Rename, OK/Cancel). f2 is the Mixer signal-flow block diagram. Each input goes Part EQ → Insertion Effect (DSP2–DSP4, DSP5, plus DSP1 when its Connection is set to Insertion) → VRM Sw. Sends then go to DSP1 (when Connection is System), CHORUS and REVERB (the System Effect block). A dry bus and the effect returns are summed and pass through MASTER COMPRESSOR → MASTER EQ. AUDIO and AUX IN join after Master EQ, at OUTPUT.

## f2 — Mixer block diagram (signal flow)
- Shows: A signal-flow block diagram of the Mixer. Signals flow left to right. The input rows are horizontal lines. Effect sends are knobs, and junction dots connect them to the rows. Thick lines carry the effect returns and the summed bus to the output. The source image is low-resolution. All labels below were readable when zoomed.
- On-screen text (verbatim):
  - Input row labels, top to bottom: "MAIN", "LAYER", "LEFT", "SONG CH1", "⋮", "SONG CH16", "STYLE RHY1", "⋮", "STYLE PHR2", "MIC", "AUDIO", "AUX IN".
  - Block labels: "PART EQ" (×7, orange), "DSP2", "DSP3", "DSP4", "DSP5", "DSP1" (appears twice), "MIC EFFECT", "VRM", "CHORUS", "REVERB", "MASTER COMPRESSOR" (green), "MASTER EQ" (orange).
  - Group labels: "INSERTION EFFECT", "SYSTEM EFFECT".
  - Switch labels: "VRM Sw" (×7); "VOCAL" and "TALK" (the two positions of the mic switch).
  - Knob labels: "DSP1 DEPTH", "CHORUS DEPTH", "REVERB DEPTH", "VARIATION RETURN LEVEL", "CHORUS RETURN LEVEL", "REVERB RETURN LEVEL".
  - Annotation (upper left of centre): "DSP1" / "Connection = System Effect" / "Connection = Insertion Effect".
  - Annotation with an arrow to DSP5: "MAIN, LAYER, LEFT, SONG CH1~16, MIC".
  - Output: "OUTPUT" with an arrowhead pointing right.
- Controls/callouts (the signal path in drawn order, left → right):
  1. **Input stage.** Every row from MAIN to STYLE PHR2 enters its own "PART EQ" block first. The rows with a PART EQ are MAIN, LAYER, LEFT, SONG CH1, SONG CH16 and STYLE RHY1. For STYLE PHR2, one PART EQ is drawn per labelled row, and "⋮" marks the channels in between.
     - MIC has no PART EQ. It goes MIC → "MIC EFFECT" → a switch with two positions, "VOCAL" (upper line) and "TALK" (lower line).
     - AUDIO and AUX IN have no blocks at all (see step 7).
     - No "Filter" block appears anywhere in the diagram.
  2. **INSERTION EFFECT block.** A dash-dot outline titled "INSERTION EFFECT" sits after the PART EQs. It contains four dashed columns, one after another, in this order:
     - The DSP2 column: the "DSP2" block is drawn on the LAYER row.
     - The DSP3 column: "DSP3" is drawn at about the LEFT row.
     - The DSP4 column: "DSP4" is drawn below SONG CH1, in the "⋮" area.
     - The DSP5 column.
     - The DSP2, DSP3 and DSP4 columns span only the rows from MAIN to SONG CH16. The STYLE rows run under them.
     - The DSP5 column spans every row from MAIN down to the TALK line. The dash-dot outline extends down around it. "DSP5" is drawn on the VOCAL (MIC) row. Its arrowed annotation reads "MAIN, LAYER, LEFT, SONG CH1~16, MIC".
     - The staggered block positions suggest each DSP can sit on one of the rows its column crosses. The diagram does not state this in words.
  3. **DSP1 as an insertion effect.** A separate dashed column comes after the Insertion Effect outline. It spans the rows from SONG CH1 down to STYLE PHR2, and a "DSP1" block is drawn on the STYLE RHY1 row. The arrow from "Connection = Insertion Effect" points to this column.
  4. **VRM.** Next, each of these 7 rows has a "VRM Sw": MAIN, LAYER, LEFT, SONG CH1, SONG CH16, STYLE RHY1 and STYLE PHR2.
     - Each switch's upper contact runs up into the light-blue "VRM" block (7 upward arrows into VRM). Its lower contact continues the row to the right.
     - The VRM output leaves from the right side of the block and becomes its own send row, just above the MAIN row.
     - The VOCAL/MIC row has no VRM Sw.
     - After the DSP1 insertion column, the TALK line turns up and joins the VOCAL row at a junction.
  5. **Effect sends (knobs with upward arrows into each effect).** From left to right:
     - "DSP1" block (top), fed through 5 knobs labelled "DSP1 DEPTH". The arrow from "Connection = System Effect" points to this block. It sits outside the "SYSTEM EFFECT" outline. Its junctions are on these rows: VRM output, SONG CH1, SONG CH16, STYLE RHY1 and STYLE PHR2. It has no send junctions on MAIN, LAYER, LEFT or VOCAL.
     - "CHORUS" block, fed through 9 knobs labelled "CHORUS DEPTH". Its junctions are on these rows, in order: VRM output, MAIN, LAYER, LEFT, SONG CH1, SONG CH16, STYLE RHY1, STYLE PHR2 and VOCAL.
     - "REVERB" block, fed through 9 knobs labelled "REVERB DEPTH". Its junctions are on the same 9 rows, in the same order.
     - One dash-dot outline titled "SYSTEM EFFECT" encloses CHORUS and REVERB only.
  6. **Returns and summing.**
     - From the top of each effect, a thick line rises through its knob: DSP1 through "VARIATION RETURN LEVEL", CHORUS through "CHORUS RETURN LEVEL" and REVERB through "REVERB RETURN LEVEL".
     - These lines join a thick top line at ⊕ junctions (the chorus return and the reverb return each have a ⊕). The top line runs right and then down the far right.
     - Separately, all 9 rows end at junction dots on a thick vertical dry bus. That bus runs down and turns at the bottom to join the same far-right thick line.
     - The two meet at a ⊕ summing point.
  7. **Master section.** The ⊕ summing point feeds "MASTER COMPRESSOR", then "MASTER EQ", then a final ⊕, then the arrow to "OUTPUT".
     - The AUDIO and AUX IN lines run the full width along the bottom and join this final ⊕ from below.
     - So AUDIO and AUX IN are drawn joining after MASTER COMPRESSOR and MASTER EQ.
- Relates to text: The "Block Diagram" heading of the Mixer chapter. The diagram shows where Part EQ, the Insertion Effects (DSP1–DSP5), VRM, the system effects (DSP1 / Chorus / Reverb), the Master Compressor and the Master EQ sit in the signal flow.

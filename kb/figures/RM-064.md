# Figures on RM p.64 — Calling Up Registration Memory Numbers in Order (Registration Sequence)
source: DGX-670_reference_manual_En_B0.pdf p.64 | described: 2026-09-29 | by: claude-opus-5-5
summary: f1 is the Regist Sequence/Freeze display with the Registration Sequence tab open. Its Sequence Data field shows "1 2 3 4 1", then the cursor, then empty dot slots (two rows of 16 positions, with "Stop" at the end). So registration number 1 appears twice in the example sequence. Button callouts 7 (Seq.), 5 (Replace/Insert/Delete) and 6 (Sequence End) point to the matching steps.

## f1 — Regist Sequence/Freeze display, Registration Sequence tab (Sequence Data programming)
- Shows: An LCD screen shot. Below it is the row of eight [1▲▼]-[8▲▼] buttons, drawn as two rows of square buttons. Buttons 2 and 8 are greyed out. Light-blue brackets group the buttons, and bold step-number callouts appear beneath them.
- On-screen text (verbatim):
  - Title bar: "Regist Sequence/Freeze" (with a small bank/file icon at the left).
  - Tabs: "Registration Sequence" (highlighted orange, selected), "Freeze".
  - Left: "Bank :" / "Bank 1".
  - Right: "Pedal" / "Regist Prev. [  Off  ]" / "Regist Next [  Right  ]".
  - "Sequence Data" field. It is a black box with two rows of slots.
    - Row 1, left to right: "1", "2", "3", "4", "1", then a solid light-grey block (the cursor, an empty position), then 10 dots ("·").
    - Row 2: 16 dots, then "Stop" at the right end.
    - Row 1 therefore has 16 positions (5 numbers + cursor + 10 dots), and row 2 has 16 dot positions. That is 32 slot positions visible in total, followed by "Stop".
  - "Data Edit" (label above Replace/Insert/Delete).
  - Bottom menu captions: "Seq." (orange, above button 1); blank above button 2; "Replace" (3); "Insert" (4); "Delete" (5); a "Sequence End" box spanning 6-7 with three indicator dots (first filled, two hollow: ● ○ ○) and the value "Stop"; "Pedal" (8).
  - Button numbers under the screen: 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 (2 and 8 shown greyed).
- Controls/callouts:
  - Light-blue bracket around the Sequence Data field → side caption: "Indicates the Registration Memory numbers, in the order of the current Registration Sequence."
  - Bracket around [1▲▼] (Seq.) → callout 7 (step 7: turn the Registration Sequence function on).
  - Bracket around [3▲▼]-[5▲▼] (Replace / Insert / Delete) → callout 5 (step 5: program the order).
  - Bracket around [6▲▼]-[7▲▼] (Sequence End, currently "Stop") → callout 6 (step 6: choose Stop / Top / Next Bank).
  - [8▲▼] "Pedal" and [2▲▼] (blank) have no callout.
- Relates to text: This illustrates step 5, "Program the Sequence order, from left to right". Use REGISTRATION MEMORY [1]-[4] plus [4▲▼] Insert, and move the cursor with [▲][▼][◀][▶]. The Replace/Insert/Delete table below the figure describes buttons [3▲▼]-[5▲▼]. Callouts 6 and 7 tie to steps 6 (Sequence End) and 7 (Seq. on). The example data "1 2 3 4 1" shows the same Registration Memory number (1) entered more than once. The figure prints no numeric maximum sequence length; the display simply shows 32 slot positions.

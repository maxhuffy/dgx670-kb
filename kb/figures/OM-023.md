## f1 — Main display with numbered callouts (1)-(9)
- Shows: LCD screenshot of the Main display (the display that appears at power-on). Layout from top to bottom:
  - Row 1 (thin black status row across the top): four boxed items, left to right: tempo, bar/beat, "Mic" meter (centre), transpose (far right).
  - Row 2 (full-width dark-grey bar directly under the status row and directly above the Voice area): the Registration Memory area.
  - Row 3 (large, full-width, light panel): the Main Voice.
  - Row 4 (middle band, split in two halves): Style panel on the left half; Layer Voice (upper) and Left Voice (lower) stacked on the right half.
  - Row 5 (bottom band, split in two halves): MIDI Song panel on the left, Audio panel on the right.
- On-screen text (verbatim):
  - Status row: "♩= 97" | bar-line icon + "001.1" | "Mic" + horizontal level-meter bar | icon (vertical bars with a note, flanked by small ‹ › arrows) + "0"

  - Main Voice panel: voice picture (grand piano), small label "VRM", large name "CFX Grand", tag "Main" at top-right.
  - Layer panel (right half, upper): strings picture, "Strings", tag "Layer".

  - Style panel (left half, middle): "Style  4/4  ♩= 97  [padlock icon]  1/2"; black category icon reading "Pop"; name "90sGuitarPop"; "D♯m" at the panel's lower-right.

  - Audio panel (bottom-right): "Audio  03:02/04:55  [repeat-arrows icon]  ● REC"; waveform icon; "Audio_001".

Left to right as drawn: (1) USB TO HOST at the far left, then (2) AUX PEDAL, (3) PEDAL UNIT, (4) MIC INPUT, (5) AUX IN, (6) PHONES/OUTPUT, and (7) DC IN at the far right in its own separate box. f2 shows the music rest being lowered into the top panel. ## f1 — Rear Panel connector drawing, callouts (1)-(7)
- Shows: A line drawing of the rear-panel connector strip, seen face-on. An outer outline runs across the full width with rounded upper corners. Its two side lines continue downward and are cut off, so the bottom is open. Inside it are two separate recessed sections:
  - Main section: a large rounded rectangle covering most of the width from the left. It holds callouts (1)-(6) in two clusters: (1)(2)(3) at its left end and (4)(5)(6) at its right end, with a wide blank gap between (3) and (4).
  - DC section: a small, separate rounded rectangle at the far right, holding only (7).
- Left-to-right order as drawn: (1) USB TO HOST → (2) AUX PEDAL → (3) PEDAL UNIT → [wide gap] → (4) MIC INPUT → (5) AUX IN → (6) PHONES/OUTPUT → [end of main section] → (7) DC IN (separate box).
  - Far left of the drawing: (1) [USB TO HOST] terminal.
  - Far right of the drawing: (7) DC IN jack.
  - Note: the extracted "Figure text: (7) (1) (2) (3) (4) (5) (6)" on the page file is the PDF text-extraction order, not the visual order. In the drawing, (7) is at the far right.
- Viewpoint: The figure does not state or show one. There are no keys, no instrument body beyond the panel frame, and no "front"/"rear" or left/right text. The only cue is the page heading "Rear Panel". Each label is printed upright beneath its connector, so the drawing presents the panel face-on. This figure alone does not show whether its left side is the player's left or right.
- On-screen text (verbatim, printed below each connector, left to right): "USB" + USB trident symbol / "TO HOST"; "AUX PEDAL"; "PEDAL UNIT"; "MIC INPUT"; "AUX IN"; "PHONES/" / "OUTPUT"; "16V" + DC symbol (solid line over dashed line) / polarity symbol ("+" left, circle symbol in the middle, "−" right). Callout numbers (1)-(7) are printed as black filled circles above each connector.
- Controls/callouts:
  - (1) → square receptacle with an inner rounded-square shape. Label "USB TO HOST". Leftmost connector; text: "[USB TO HOST] terminal … page 92".
  - (2) → round jack (concentric circles). Label "AUX PEDAL". Text: "[AUX PEDAL] jack … see below".
  - (3) → larger round multi-pin connector, with a small mark that looks like a circled × directly above it, just under the callout number. Label "PEDAL UNIT". Last connector of the left cluster. Text: "[PEDAL UNIT] jack … page 15".
  - (4) → round jack (concentric circles). Label "MIC INPUT". First connector of the right cluster. Text: "[MIC INPUT] jack … page 77".
  - (5) → round jack (concentric circles). Label "AUX IN". Text: "[AUX IN] jack … page 95".
  - (6) → round jack (concentric circles). Label "PHONES/OUTPUT". Rightmost connector inside the main section. Text: "[PHONES/OUTPUT] jack … page 98".
  - (7) → square-framed round socket in its own separate box at the far right. Label "16V" with DC and polarity symbols. Text: "DC IN jack … page 16" ("For connecting the AC adaptor").
- Relates to text: Illustrates the whole "Rear Panel" list on p.14, items (1)-(7), including each item's cross-reference page. It also sits above the CAUTION box about turning off power and setting volumes to minimum (0) before connecting other equipment.

## f2 — Attaching the Music Rest

The music rest is drawn above the instrument, with two thick black arrows pointing down from its lower edge onto the top surface. The arrows land behind the control-panel area, between the two shaded rectangular areas at the left and right ends of the top surface. The full keyboard is visible along the front edge.
- On-screen text (verbatim): none.
- Controls/callouts: No numbered callouts. The two downward arrows show where the music rest's lower edge is inserted. The slots themselves are not drawn as separate features.
- Relates to text: "Attaching the Music Rest — Insert the music rest into the slots as shown."

## extra — Footswitch connected to the AUX PEDAL jack
- Shows: A line drawing of a footswitch pedal with a grid-textured top surface. A small rotated wordmark on the pedal appears to read "YAMAHA". Its cable runs to the right and ends in a straight phone-type plug, which is drawn pointing into a round jack (concentric circles).
- On-screen text (verbatim): "AUX PEDAL" (under the jack); pedal wordmark appears to read "YAMAHA" (small, rotated).
- Controls/callouts: No numbered callouts. The footswitch plug → [AUX PEDAL] jack.
- Relates to text: The "Connecting a Footswitch" paragraph. The included footswitch connects to the [AUX PEDAL] jack. Separately sold FC3A, FC4A or FC5 pedals can also connect there. Sustain is assigned by default. The NOTEs say to keep the power off when connecting or disconnecting, and not to press the footswitch while turning the power on (this reverses its polarity). The "Assigning a function to each pedal" box points to the Pedal tab of the Controller display via [MENU] (page 100).

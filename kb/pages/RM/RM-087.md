Speaker
Determines whether or not the sound is output from the speaker of this instrument.

• Headphone Switch: Speaker sounds normally, but is cut off when head- phones or an external device is connected to the [PHONES/OUTPUT] jack.
• On: Speaker sound is always on, even if headphones are connected.
• Off: Speaker sound is off. You can only hear the instrument sound via the headphones or an external device connected to the [PHONES/OUT- PUT] jack.

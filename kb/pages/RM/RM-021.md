## Learning How To Play Specific Chords (Chord Tutor)

If you know the name of a chord but don’t know how to play it, the Chord Tutor function conveniently shows you which notes to play.

1. Call up the operation display.
[MENU] → Cursor buttons [▲][▼][◀][▶] Chord Tutor, [ENTER]

2. Use the [1▲▼] /[2▲▼] (Root) buttons to select the chord root, and use the [3▲▼] / [4▲▼] (Type) buttons to select the chord type.
The notes you need to play are shown in the display.

NOTE
Depending on the chord, some notes may be omitted.

# RM p.71 — Editing EQ/Master EQ (Equalizer) Parameters

## Editing EQ/Master EQ (Equalizer) Parameters

Equalizer (also called “EQ”) is a sound processor that divides the frequency spectrum into multiple bands that can be boosted or cut as required to tailor the overall frequency response. The EQ page selected by using the TAB [◀][▶] buttons lets you adjust the EQ for each corresponding part, while the Master EQ page lets you make overall EQ adjustments for the entire instrument. The Master EQ
page can also be called up directly from any display by pressing the [MIXER/EQ] and [CHANNEL ON/ OFF] buttons simultaneously.

## Editing Part EQ

[FIG RM-071-f1]

Move the Cursor on the display by using the Cursor buttons [▲][▼][◀][▶], and then use the Data dial or the [1▲▼]–[8▲▼] buttons to edit parameters.

High
Boosts or attenuates the high EQ band for each part.

Low
Boosts or attenuates the low EQ band for each part.

## Editing Master EQ

The instrument possesses a high-quality five-band digital EQ. With this function, a final effect—tone control—can be added to the output of your instrument. You can select one of the five preset EQ settings in the Master EQ page. You can even create your own custom EQ settings by adjusting the frequency bands, and save the settings as User Master EQ types.

NOTE
Master EQ cannot be applied to playback of audio received via the USB Audio Player function or the Audio Input Sounds.

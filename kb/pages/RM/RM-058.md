## Making and Saving the Microphone Settings

This section lets you set parameters for various Effects that are applied to the microphone sound. You should make both Vocal and Talk settings—Vocal, for your singing performance, and Talk, for making announcements between songs, for example.

1. Call up the operation display.
[MIC SETTING] → TAB [▶] Setting

2. Press the [1▲] (Vocal) or [1▼] (Talk) button to select the desired setting display.

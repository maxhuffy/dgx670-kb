## Top Panel

(1) [⏻] (Standby/On) switch .................. page 16
Turns on the instrument’s power or sets to standby.

(2) [MASTER VOLUME] dial ................. page 17
Adjusts the overall volume.

(3) [DEMO] button.................................. page 11
Plays the Demos.

(4) SONG related buttons........................ page 60
For selecting Songs and controlling Song playback.

(5) STYLE category selection buttons..... page 48
For selecting a Style category.

(6) [DIRECT ACCESS] button................ page 22
Instantly calls up the desired display with just a single additional button press.

(7) [METRONOME] button.................... page 44
Turns the metronome on or off.

(8) [TEMPO/TAP] button ....................... page 52
Controls the tempo for Style, Song and Metronome playback.

(9) TRANSPOSE [-]/[+] buttons ............ page 44
Transposes the entire pitch of the instrument in semitone steps.

(10) [CHANNEL ON/OFF] button ....pages 53, 65

Calls up settings for turning Style/Song channels on or off.

(11) [MIXER/EQ] button.......................... page 88

Calls up various settings for the keyboard, Style and Song parts.

(12) [OTS LINK] button ........................... page 52

Turns on/off the OTS Link function.

(13) STYLE CONTROL buttons ............... page 48

Controls Style playback.

(14) LCD and related controls .................. page 19

Indicates the current panel settings and allows you to select or change the settings.

(15) [MENU] button................................ page 100

Lets you make advanced settings and create your original Styles and Songs.

If your instrument is equipped with Bluetooth functionality (page 96), the Bluetooth logo is printed on the panel button. Depending on the country in which you purchased the product, the instrument may not have Bluetooth capability.

# OM p.99 — Connecting a Foot Pedal ([AUX PEDAL] jack); Connecting a Pedal Unit ([PEDAL UNIT] jack)

## Connecting a Foot Pedal ([AUX PEDAL] jack)

The included footswitch or other separately sold foot pedals (FC3A, FC4A or FC5) can be connected to the [AUX PEDAL] jack. For details, refer to page 14.

NOTE
• Make sure that power is off when connecting or disconnecting the pedal.
• Do not press the footswitch while turning the power on. Doing this changes the recognized polarity of the pedal, resulting in reversed operation.

## Connecting a Pedal Unit ([PEDAL UNIT] jack)

The separately sold LP-1B or LP-1WH pedal unit can be connected to the [PEDAL UNIT] jack. For details, refer to page 15.

NOTE
• Make sure that power is off when connecting or disconnecting the pedal.
• Do not press the pedals while turning the power on. Doing this changes the recognized polarity of the pedals, resulting in reversed operation.

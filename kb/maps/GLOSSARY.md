# Glossary — everyday wording → manual terms

Maps how people ask to the terms the manuals actually use. Then look the manual term up in
`TERMS.md` / `BUTTONS.md` / `MENU_PATHS.md` / `TOC.md` for pages.
Every term in the right column was checked to appear in the manuals. **This file grows:** when a
question's wording didn't map and you found the right term, add a row (keep alphabetical by first phrase).

|---|---|

| bass with left hand, chords with right hand | Specifying Chords with Your Right Hand while Playing Bass with Your Left Hand (RM Ch3) |

| computer, DAW, MIDI | [USB TO HOST]; MIDI; Connections chapter |
| demo songs | Demo; [DEMO] |

| edit a sound | Voice Set (Editing Voices) |
